Hawk UAV landing trainer

Bring a 24,000 lb HALE drone down at one of four airfields. You start 5 nm out on final at 1,800 ft and 140 kt; the map in the corner shows every runway and you can retarget mid-flight by clicking one. Configure flaps and gear, slow to Vref, hold the 3° glideslope, flare, and stop before the end. The TF-10K-D takes about eight seconds from idle to full thrust, so keep the engine spooled.
