How to play

Landing in five steps

  1. Slow down. Pull the throttle back toward 30 % and let the speed fall below 160 kt.
  2. Configure. Flaps 30 and gear down by 2 nm out. Check three green gear lights.
  3. Ride the glideslope. Keep the PAPI lights two white, two red, and the pink ILS needles centred.
  4. Hold Vref. About 127 kt at roughly 10° angle of attack. The engine needs 8 s to spool up, so stay ahead of it.
  5. Flare and stop. At 20 ft raise the nose a couple of degrees, idle the throttle, then brakes and speed brake.

Too busy? Press APP with AP and A/THR on and the autopilot lands it for you.

Taking off

  1. Release the brakes. Press B (or flip BRAKES in the cockpit) and push the throttle to TO/GA.
  2. Keep it straight. Steer with Q and E (or the arrow keys) as the speed builds.
  3. Rotate at Vr. At about 124 kt, shown on the HUD and the speed tape, pull back smoothly to 10° nose-up. Past 13° the tail scrapes.
  4. Clean up. It lifts off around 140 kt. At "positive rate" raise the gear (G), then flaps up (F) above 180 kt.

After a landing you can also add full power before you stop and rotate again for a touch-and-go.

Aerobatics

The H-20 flies like a fighter: roll it inverted, loop it, pull a 9 g turn. Climb to 8,000 ft at full power and over 350 kt first. A full-stick loop from 400 kt takes about 16 s. Pull too hard when slow and it stalls; hard turns bleed speed fast.
