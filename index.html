Hawk UAV landing trainer

Bring a 12,800 lb canard-delta unmanned fighter down at one of four airfields. You start 5 nm out on final at 1,800 ft and 160 kt; the map in the corner shows every runway and you can retarget mid-flight by clicking one. Configure flaps and gear, slow to Vref, hold the 3° glideslope, flare, and stop before the end. The delta wing approaches at about 10° angle of attack, so a big flare scrapes the tail. The TF-10K takes about eight seconds from idle to full thrust.
