Hawk UAV landing trainer

Bring a 24,000 lb HALE drone down on runway 36 at Test Range Delta. You are 5 nm out at 1,800 ft and 140 kt. Configure flaps and gear, slow to Vref, hold the 3° glideslope, flare, and stop before the end. The TF-10K-D takes about eight seconds from idle to full thrust, so keep the engine spooled.
